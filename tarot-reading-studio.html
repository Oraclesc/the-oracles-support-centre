Learn by doing

Guided Tarot Reading Studio

Use this guided workspace to practise interpreting cards thoughtfully. Choose a question and spread, explore a sample set of cards, connect the card positions and finish with your own reflection. The built-in example is for learning; it is not a prediction.

Read the Tarot Workbook Explore Card Meanings

1. Set your intention

Tip: use open questions that help you reflect. Tarot cannot verify facts, guarantee future events or replace professional medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice.

2. Explore the example cards

Choose a spread and begin to see a fictional practice example.

3. Connect the interpretation

Work through these prompts. First describe what you notice in the images; then compare that observation with traditional meanings and the card's position.

4. Learn from your interpretation

  • Could a card have another reasonable meaning?
  • Have I separated what I can see from what I assume?
  • Am I using the reading to explore options rather than make a decision for me?
  • What real-world information or conversation would help me understand the situation better?
