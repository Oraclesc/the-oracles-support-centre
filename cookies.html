Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to provide functionality, understand how visitors use the site, manage privacy choices and support advertising.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function correctly, remember preferences and collect information about website usage.

How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

  • Website functionality and performance.
  • Website analytics through Google Analytics.
  • Advertising and advertising measurement through Google AdSense.
  • Consent and privacy-choice management where enabled.
  • Fraud prevention and security associated with third-party services.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits, interactions, devices, browsers and general website usage. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Google AdSense

Google AdSense and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising, prevent fraud and understand advertising performance. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Where required, personalised advertising is subject to the consent choices collected through the website's consent-management system. You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings and learn about opting out of some third-party vendors at aboutads.info.

UK, EEA and Switzerland

Visitors in the UK, European Economic Area and Switzerland may be shown a consent message when consent is required for advertising or analytics. Consent choices can affect the use of personalised advertising and certain analytics functions.

Managing Cookies

You can control, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block cookies, some website features or services may not work as intended.
