Weekly Tarot practice

Keep the same card theme in view for seven days. Each day, record one concrete observation that connects with the theme and one thing that does not. This makes the practice about observation rather than confirmation.

Monday

What theme deserves attention?

Midweek

Where has the theme appeared in ordinary life?

Weekend

What did you learn that the card did not predict?

Daily Tarot safety check

Use daily readings as prompts, not promises. Avoid using a card as the sole basis for medical, legal, financial or safety-critical decisions.
