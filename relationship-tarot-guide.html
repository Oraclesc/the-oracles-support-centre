Relationship Tarot Guide

Relationship readings are most useful when they help you examine communication, expectations, boundaries and your own choices.

1

Start With Your Perspective

Ask what you want to understand and what you can influence. Focus on your own experience rather than trying to prove another person's private thoughts.

2

Communication

Tarot can prompt questions about how clearly you communicate and what conversation needs attention. A card is not a substitute for having that conversation.

3

Boundaries

Use readings to explore what you need from a healthy connection and where clearer limits may help.

4

After a Breakup

Focus on recovery, patterns, boundaries and what you want to learn rather than repeatedly asking whether an ex will return.

5

Three-Card Relationship Spread

Card 1: what I bring. Card 2: what deserves attention. Card 3: what could support healthier communication or boundaries.

6

Useful Questions

What pattern should I notice? How can I communicate more clearly? What boundary needs attention? What am I assuming rather than knowing?
