How we create our guides

Editorial Standards and Tarot Learning Method

This page explains the approach used for educational material on The Oracle's Support Centre. Our aim is to help readers learn tarot thoughtfully, understand the reasoning behind an interpretation and make their own decisions rather than rely on a card as an instruction.

Last reviewed: 28 October 2026

How interpretations are presented

Tarot meanings vary between decks, authors and traditions. Where a guide describes a card, it presents themes commonly associated with the Rider–Waite–Smith tradition as interpretive prompts, not as universal facts. A card's imagery, the question asked, its spread position and surrounding cards can all change how a reader understands it.

  • We distinguish traditional themes from a possible interpretation of a particular question.
  • Where useful, we include an alternative reading so that one meaning is not treated as the only answer.
  • We encourage readers to notice visual details, explain their reasoning and consider what evidence or context they may be missing.
  • Examples labelled fictional are teaching scenarios, not testimonials or reports about real people.

What tarot can and cannot do

This site approaches tarot as a symbolic, reflective and educational practice. A reading cannot verify another person's private thoughts, establish facts, guarantee an outcome or replace practical research. We encourage readers to use their judgement and seek qualified help for medical, legal, financial or mental-health matters.

How we develop and review content

Guides are organised around learning tasks such as understanding card imagery, asking clearer questions, choosing a spread and connecting several cards into a coherent interpretation. Interactive tools are intended to support practice; their sample meanings are prompts rather than personalised professional advice.

When content is revised, we aim to improve clarity, correct errors, remove misleading certainty and make navigation more useful. We do not claim that a page has been independently fact-checked or reviewed by a qualified specialist unless that is explicitly stated on the page.

Sources, originality and limitations

Tarot is a tradition with differing schools of interpretation. Our educational explanations are written for this site and should be read as an introduction, not as the definitive account of every tradition. Historical claims and specialist topics should be supported by reliable references; where a page does not provide a source list, readers should treat it as introductory material and consult additional books or reputable institutions for further study.

Card meanings are not presented as scientifically established predictions. We aim to be transparent about uncertainty and to avoid language that pressures readers into a particular decision.

Corrections and feedback

If you find an error, an unclear explanation, a broken link or an accessibility problem, please contact us with the page URL and a description of the issue. We welcome constructive suggestions for making the learning material more useful.
