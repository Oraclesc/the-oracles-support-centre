7-Day and 30-Day Tarot Practice Challenges

Practice works best when it is small enough to continue.

1

Seven-Day Challenge

Day 1: look through all 78 cards. Day 2: study one Major Arcana card. Day 3: compare one suit. Day 4: complete a one-card reading. Day 5: try three cards. Day 6: compare two cards. Day 7: review your notes.

2

Thirty-Day Plan

Days 1–5: deck structure and Major Arcana. Days 6–10: suits, numbers and court cards. Days 11–15: reversals and symbolism. Days 16–20: spreads. Days 21–25: combinations. Days 26–28: create a spread and complete readings. Days 29–30: review your journal.

3

Five-Minute Routine

Choose one question, draw one card, study the image, write three observations, check the traditional meaning and write one practical reflection.

4

If You Miss a Day

Start again the next day. Do not compensate with extra cards. The challenge is a learning structure, not a test.
