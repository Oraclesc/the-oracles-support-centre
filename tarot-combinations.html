Tarot Card Combinations

Two cards can be read as a conversation. One may describe the situation while the other changes, challenges, supports or develops it. Choose any two cards below.

There is no single universal meaning for a pair. Start with the question, consider each card's role, then notice what changes when you reverse their order.
