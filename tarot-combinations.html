Tarot Card Combinations

Two cards can be read as a conversation. One may describe the situation while the other changes, challenges, supports or develops it. Choose any two cards below.

How to Read Two Tarot Cards Together

A combination is not simply the definition of card one plus the definition of card two. Read the pair in relation to the question and the position of each card. One card can describe a situation while the second adds context, tension, movement or a different point of view.

Look for agreement and contrast

Notice repeated suits, numbers, elements and visual themes. Then look for differences. A card associated with movement beside one associated with patience creates a different conversation from two cards that both emphasise rapid change. Neither pair has one universal answer; context determines what is useful.

Try reversing the order

Read the first card as the starting point and the second as the influence on it. Then swap them. If the emphasis changes, you have found something worth considering about sequence and perspective. This is a study exercise rather than a rule that every reader must follow.
