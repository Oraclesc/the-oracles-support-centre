Using Tarot to Reflect on Work and Career

A career reading can be a useful way to organise thoughts about work, motivation and priorities. It is not a substitute for researching an employer, checking a contract, understanding your finances or taking professional career advice. The value of the exercise is in the questions it helps you notice.

Four practical perspectives

Use the positions to consider where you are now, what is creating difficulty, what opportunity deserves attention and what you can focus on next. After drawing the cards, write one concrete observation for each position. Then ask which observations are supported by facts you already know and which are simply possibilities worth exploring.

Move from symbolism to action

A card about patience might prompt you to review whether you have given a process enough time; a card about change might encourage you to investigate options rather than assume that change is inevitable. Keep the distinction clear between the symbolic prompt and the real-world evidence. The final decision remains yours.
