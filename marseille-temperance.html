Temperance

Tempérance · Major Arcana

Temperance depicts the careful movement and blending of forces. It favours adjustment, exchange and a process that develops over time.

Tarot de Marseille

French title: Tempérance

Keywords: moderation, exchange, adjustment

Browse the Marseille deck

Upright Meaning

Temperance depicts the careful movement and blending of forces. It favours adjustment, exchange and a process that develops over time.

Reversed Meaning

Imbalance, impatience or incompatible elements may be difficult to reconcile. Change the proportions gradually and observe the result.

What This Card Brings Into Focus

As a Marseille trump, Tempérance is best approached through its own image, number 14, and place in the sequence. Its key tension is moderation, exchange, adjustment.

Love & Relationships

Temperance brings moderation into the relationship question. Consider the choices, values and patterns that shape the connection. The card cannot establish another person's private thoughts; use it to reflect on observable behaviour and honest communication.

Career & Work

Temperance asks how moderation affects practical decisions. For work, study or major projects, consider direction, responsibility and the wider stage of development.

Personal Growth

For personal reflection, consider how moderation challenges your current approach. What value, habit or choice is being brought into view?

Symbolism & Visual Study

Study the actual Marseille image of Tempérance before importing meanings from a modern illustrated deck. Notice the figures, posture, objects, colours and direction. In particular, test how the image expresses moderation, exchange, adjustment.

Reading the Marseille Tradition

The Tarot de Marseille is a family of historical patterns, not one single immutable deck. This guide uses a Marseille-pattern 78-card image set and modern reflective interpretations. Numbered suit cards are read through pip arrangement, number and suit symbolism; they do not use the same illustrated scenes as Rider–Waite–Smith.
