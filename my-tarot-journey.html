Your Tarot Journey as a Learning Record

The journey page brings together local activity from the site's practice tools. It can help you see whether you are reading regularly, revisiting cards and recording reflections. The numbers are a record of activity on this browser, not a measure of how skilled you are at tarot.

Use progress to guide your next practice

If you have many favourites but few journal entries, spend time writing about why those cards matter to you. If you have completed lessons but rarely practise, try a simple one-card reading. If you have many readings saved, compare them for repeated questions or themes rather than repeatedly asking the same question for reassurance.

Local rather than account-based

The journey features rely on information stored locally in your browser. There is no server-side profile behind this page. Treat the dashboard as a personal practice aid and avoid entering sensitive information into local notes.
