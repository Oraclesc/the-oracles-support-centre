How to Study Tarot Card Meanings

The card search is designed to help you move between a quick lookup and a deeper study of the deck. Search by a theme such as change, communication or work, then open the individual card guide rather than stopping at a single keyword.

Start with the structure of the deck

The Major Arcana often deals with broad developmental themes, while the Minor Arcana gives more everyday context through the four suits. Numbers and court cards also provide useful patterns. When several search results share a suit, number or theme, compare them instead of treating each card as an isolated definition.

Build your own working meanings

Write down what you notice in a card before reading the guide. Then compare your observations with the traditional interpretation. Over time, this creates a personal study record without requiring you to memorise every keyword at once.
