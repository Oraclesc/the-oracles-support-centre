Tarot Journal Prompts

These prompts help turn readings into a record you can learn from.

1

Daily Practice

What should I pay attention to today? What detail did I notice first? What emotion does the image create? Where might this theme appear?

2

Card Study

What symbols stand out? How does the number affect the meaning? What does the suit contribute? How would I describe the card without keywords?

3

Reading Review

What was my exact question? What was my first impression? Which part was supported by facts? What assumption might I have brought into the reading?

4

Relationships

What do I need from this connection? What communication pattern deserves attention? What boundary would support a healthier relationship?

5

Career

What part of this opportunity needs research? What strength can I use? What practical information am I missing?

6

Personal Growth

What pattern keeps appearing? What belief might be limiting me? What strength have I overlooked? What am I ready to release?

7

Responsible Reading

Am I seeking information or reassurance? Would a real-world conversation be more useful? What would it look like to put the cards away and decide myself?
