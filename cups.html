Cups Tarot Card Meanings

The Suit of Cups is connected with emotions, relationships, intuition, love, compassion and connection. These 14 cards explore the emotional experiences that shape our lives.

The Suit of Cups

Cups are associated with the element of Water and often explore feelings, relationships, intuition and emotional experiences.

The cards can describe everything from new emotional beginnings and meaningful connections to disappointment, reflection and the need to move on.

A

Ace of Cups

Upright: New emotional beginnings, love, compassion, openness and emotional renewal.

Reversed: Emotional blockage, disappointment, difficulty expressing feelings or an opportunity that has not yet been embraced.

The Ace of Cups represents the beginning of a new emotional experience. It can suggest love, connection, compassion or a renewed ability to understand and express your feelings.

Ace of Cups in a Reading

The Ace of Cups often appears when something emotionally meaningful is beginning. This might involve a new relationship, a deeper connection with someone, personal healing or simply becoming more open to your own emotions.

It can also represent an emotional opportunity. The feeling may be present, but what you do with it is still your choice.

Ace of Cups as Advice

Allow yourself to be open to genuine emotional experiences. If you have been protecting yourself from disappointment, consider whether there is room to let yourself connect again.

Pay attention to feelings that arise naturally rather than immediately dismissing them.

Questions for Reflection

  • What new emotional possibility is appearing?
  • Am I allowing myself to receive love and support?
  • What feelings have I been holding back?
  • Where could I be more open and compassionate?

Reflection: The Ace of Cups reminds you that emotional renewal can begin with openness. Give yourself permission to feel, connect and explore what is beginning.

II

Two of Cups

Upright: Partnership, mutual attraction, connection, cooperation, trust and emotional harmony.

Reversed: Disconnection, imbalance, misunderstanding, conflict or difficulty meeting each other's needs.

The Two of Cups represents a meaningful connection between people and the mutual exchange of feelings, trust or support.

Two of Cups in a Reading

The Two of Cups often points towards a relationship in which both people are willing to meet each other with openness and respect. It can describe romantic relationships, friendships, partnerships or another important connection.

The card is particularly concerned with reciprocity. A healthy connection requires both people to participate rather than one person carrying everything alone.

Two of Cups as Advice

Look for mutual understanding. Be willing to communicate honestly while also listening to the other person's perspective.

If a relationship matters to you, consider what you can do to strengthen trust and create a healthier balance between giving and receiving.

Questions for Reflection

  • Which relationships currently feel mutually supportive?
  • Am I giving and receiving equally?
  • What would improve trust between us?
  • Am I communicating honestly about how I feel?

Reflection: The Two of Cups reminds you that meaningful relationships are built through mutual respect, honesty and willingness to connect.

III

Three of Cups

Upright: Friendship, celebration, community, support, happiness and shared experiences.

Reversed: Isolation, excess, social tension, gossip or feeling disconnected from a group.

The Three of Cups represents the joy that can come from sharing experiences with people who support and appreciate you.

Three of Cups in a Reading

The Three of Cups often points towards friendship, celebration and community. It can appear when spending time with supportive people is especially valuable.

The card can also represent a milestone worth celebrating. Not every achievement has to be experienced alone; sharing positive moments can strengthen relationships and create lasting memories.

Three of Cups as Advice

Make time for the people who bring genuine encouragement into your life. Allow yourself to celebrate progress rather than immediately moving on to the next challenge.

If you have been withdrawing from others, consider whether reconnecting with trusted friends or community could help.

Questions for Reflection

  • Who makes me feel supported and understood?
  • What achievement deserves to be celebrated?
  • Have I been making enough time for friendship?
  • Where could community help me right now?

Reflection: The Three of Cups reminds you that connection and shared joy can be important sources of strength. Celebrate the good moments and appreciate the people who share them with you.

IV

Four of Cups

Upright: Contemplation, apathy, emotional withdrawal, dissatisfaction and reconsidering what you want.

Reversed: Renewed interest, awareness of opportunities, emotional movement or choosing to engage again.

The Four of Cups can appear when you feel emotionally disconnected or dissatisfied, even when possibilities are available around you.

Four of Cups in a Reading

The Four of Cups often encourages you to look carefully at your emotional state. You may be focused so strongly on what is missing that you are overlooking something that could be valuable.

It can also represent a genuine need to pause. Not every opportunity needs to be accepted immediately. Taking time to understand what you actually want can be useful before making a decision.

Four of Cups as Advice

Give yourself time to reflect, but avoid becoming so withdrawn that you stop noticing what is happening around you.

Ask yourself whether you are genuinely dissatisfied or whether disappointment from the past is influencing how you see current possibilities.

Questions for Reflection

  • What am I currently dissatisfied with?
  • Am I overlooking an opportunity because of disappointment?
  • What do I actually want at this point?
  • Would a short pause help me understand my feelings?

Reflection: The Four of Cups reminds you to notice both your feelings and the possibilities around you. A pause can be valuable, but staying emotionally closed for too long can make it harder to recognise what may be worth accepting.

V

Five of Cups

Upright: Disappointment, grief, regret, loss and focusing on what has gone wrong.

Reversed: Acceptance, recovery, moving forward, forgiveness or beginning to recognise what remains.

The Five of Cups represents emotional disappointment and the tendency to focus on what has been lost rather than what is still available.

Five of Cups in a Reading

The Five of Cups can appear during a period of disappointment, regret or emotional difficulty. Something may not have turned out as you hoped, and it is natural to need time to process that experience.

At the same time, the card encourages you to notice what remains. Loss can be real without meaning that everything has been lost. Support, opportunities or relationships may still be available even if they are difficult to see while you are hurting.

Five of Cups as Advice

Give yourself permission to acknowledge disappointment rather than pretending everything is fine. Once you have processed what happened, gradually turn your attention towards what can still be rebuilt.

Try not to let one painful experience define your expectations for everything that comes next.

Questions for Reflection

  • What am I grieving or feeling disappointed about?
  • What remains available to me?
  • Am I allowing myself enough time to process what happened?
  • What could help me begin moving forward?

Reflection: The Five of Cups reminds you that disappointment deserves to be acknowledged, but it does not have to define the future. When you are ready, look towards what remains and what can still grow.

VI

Six of Cups

Upright: Nostalgia, memories, childhood, kindness, familiarity and emotional connection with the past.

Reversed: Living in the past, difficulty moving on, idealising memories or needing to create emotional distance from earlier experiences.

The Six of Cups connects the present with memories, familiarity and experiences from the past.

Six of Cups in a Reading

The Six of Cups can represent a return to something familiar. This might involve reconnecting with someone, remembering an earlier period of life or rediscovering something that once brought you happiness.

The card can also encourage you to consider how your past experiences influence the way you approach the present. Memories can provide comfort and insight, but they may not always show the complete picture.

Six of Cups as Advice

Allow yourself to appreciate meaningful memories without feeling that you have to recreate the past exactly as it was.

Consider what qualities from earlier experiences are worth carrying forward into your current life.

Questions for Reflection

  • What memory is influencing how I feel today?
  • What can I learn from my past without becoming stuck there?
  • Is there something from my childhood that still matters to me?
  • What positive quality from the past could I bring into the present?

Reflection: The Six of Cups reminds you that the past can offer comfort, insight and meaningful memories. Appreciate where you have come from while allowing yourself to continue growing.

VII

Seven of Cups

Upright: Choices, imagination, possibilities, temptation, dreams and uncertainty.

Reversed: Clarity, narrowing your options, realistic choices or becoming overwhelmed by possibilities.

The Seven of Cups represents a situation where many possibilities are available, but not all of them are equally realistic or beneficial.

Seven of Cups in a Reading

The Seven of Cups often appears when you have several choices or are imagining many different outcomes. Having possibilities can be exciting, but too many options can also make it difficult to decide what you actually want.

The card encourages you to distinguish between genuine opportunities and attractive ideas that may not be practical. Imagination is valuable, but decisions benefit from clear information and realistic expectations.

Seven of Cups as Advice

Give yourself time to consider your options, but avoid endlessly adding more possibilities without making a decision.

Identify what matters most to you and investigate the choices that genuinely fit your priorities.

Questions for Reflection

  • Which options are genuinely realistic?
  • Am I attracted to an idea or to what I imagine it could become?
  • Which choice best fits my priorities?
  • What information would help me make a clearer decision?

Reflection: The Seven of Cups reminds you that possibilities can be both exciting and distracting. Take time to separate imagination from reality and choose the options that genuinely deserve your energy.

VIII

Eight of Cups

Upright: Walking away, leaving behind, emotional searching, change and seeking something more meaningful.

Reversed: Fear of leaving, returning to the past, avoidance or difficulty accepting that something has changed.

The Eight of Cups represents the decision to leave something behind when it no longer provides the fulfilment or meaning you need.

Eight of Cups in a Reading

The Eight of Cups can appear when you recognise that continuing with a situation is no longer emotionally satisfying. The circumstances may not necessarily be terrible; they may simply no longer be right for where you are now.

Walking away can be difficult when something has required a lot of time, effort or emotional investment. The card asks whether continuing is genuinely helping you or whether you are staying because leaving feels uncomfortable.

Eight of Cups as Advice

Be honest about whether a situation still meets your needs. If something has reached its natural end, allowing yourself to move on may create space for a more meaningful direction.

Leaving does not always mean failure. Sometimes it means recognising that your priorities have changed.

Questions for Reflection

  • What am I staying in even though it no longer fulfils me?
  • Am I afraid to leave because of what I have already invested?
  • What would I be making space for by moving on?
  • What does a more meaningful direction look like to me?

Reflection: The Eight of Cups reminds you that moving on can sometimes be an act of self-awareness. Recognising when something no longer fulfils you can be the beginning of finding a more meaningful direction.

IX

Nine of Cups

Upright: Contentment, satisfaction, gratitude, pleasure and emotional fulfilment.

Reversed: Dissatisfaction, overindulgence, unrealistic expectations or discovering that an achievement has not brought the happiness expected.

The Nine of Cups is often associated with wishes, satisfaction and appreciating what you already have.

Nine of Cups in a Reading

The Nine of Cups can appear when you are reaching a point of emotional satisfaction or when something you hoped for is beginning to take shape.

It also encourages you to consider what happiness actually means to you. Achieving a goal can feel wonderful, but lasting fulfilment often comes from appreciating the experience rather than constantly looking for the next thing to achieve.

Nine of Cups as Advice

Take time to appreciate what is already going well. Recognise your achievements and allow yourself to enjoy the things that bring genuine satisfaction.

If you are pursuing something you believe will make you happy, consider whether the goal itself reflects what you truly value.

Questions for Reflection

  • What am I genuinely grateful for?
  • What would emotional fulfilment look like for me?
  • Am I allowing myself to enjoy what I have achieved?
  • Am I expecting something external to provide all of my happiness?

Reflection: The Nine of Cups reminds you to appreciate genuine satisfaction and recognise the good things already present in your life. Fulfilment can begin with gratitude as much as achievement.

X

Ten of Cups

Upright: Emotional fulfilment, harmony, family, belonging, happiness and lasting connection.

Reversed: Family tension, emotional disconnection, unrealistic expectations or difficulty creating harmony.

The Ten of Cups represents a deep sense of emotional connection, belonging and shared happiness.

Ten of Cups in a Reading

The Ten of Cups often points towards supportive relationships and a sense of emotional belonging. It can represent family, close friendships, partnership or a community where people feel accepted and valued.

The card does not require a perfect life. Instead, it suggests that meaningful happiness can come from relationships built on care, acceptance and shared values.

Ten of Cups as Advice

Invest in the relationships that genuinely matter to you. Consider what creates a sense of belonging and how you can contribute to an environment where people feel supported.

Try not to compare your emotional life with an imagined picture of perfection. Focus on the connections that are real and meaningful to you.

Questions for Reflection

  • Where do I feel most accepted and understood?
  • What relationships bring genuine emotional fulfilment?
  • How can I contribute to greater harmony around me?
  • Am I comparing my life with an unrealistic idea of happiness?

Reflection: The Ten of Cups reminds you that lasting happiness can grow from connection, acceptance and shared experiences. Appreciate the people and relationships that give your life a sense of belonging.

P

Page of Cups

Upright: Curiosity, emotional openness, imagination, sensitivity, creativity and new emotional experiences.

Reversed: Emotional immaturity, insecurity, sensitivity, disappointment or difficulty expressing feelings.

The Page of Cups represents a gentle and curious approach to emotions, intuition and creativity.

Page of Cups in a Reading

The Page of Cups can represent someone who approaches life with emotional curiosity and imagination. It may describe a person, an attitude or the beginning of a new emotional or creative experience.

The card can also point towards messages or unexpected emotional developments. Staying open-minded may help you recognise possibilities that a more guarded approach could overlook.

Page of Cups as Advice

Allow yourself to be curious about your feelings. You do not need to have everything figured out before exploring an emotional or creative idea.

Approach yourself and others with patience rather than judging emotions simply because they are unfamiliar or difficult to explain.

Questions for Reflection

  • What feeling or idea is asking for my attention?
  • Where could I be more open-minded?
  • Am I allowing myself enough room for creativity?
  • What would happen if I approached this with curiosity instead of judgement?

Reflection: The Page of Cups reminds you that emotional awareness can begin with simple curiosity. Stay open to new feelings, ideas and experiences without demanding immediate certainty.

KN

Knight of Cups

Upright: Romance, emotional expression, idealism, charm, imagination and following the heart.

Reversed: Emotional instability, unrealistic idealism, moodiness, disappointment or being carried away by fantasy.

The Knight of Cups represents movement guided by emotion, imagination and personal values.

Knight of Cups in a Reading

The Knight of Cups can appear when emotions are motivating you to take action. It may represent romance, an invitation, a creative pursuit or the decision to follow something that feels personally meaningful.

The Knight's enthusiasm can be inspiring, but it can also become idealistic. Strong feelings are valuable, but practical considerations still matter when making important decisions.

Knight of Cups as Advice

Listen to your feelings and allow yourself to pursue what genuinely matters to you. At the same time, make sure your expectations are grounded in reality.

Express yourself honestly rather than waiting for someone else to understand what you feel without being told.

Questions for Reflection

  • What is motivating me emotionally?
  • Am I following something meaningful or simply an idealised fantasy?
  • What feelings do I need to express?
  • How can I balance emotion with practical judgement?

Reflection: The Knight of Cups reminds you that emotions and imagination can inspire meaningful action. Follow what matters to you while keeping your expectations grounded.

Q

Queen of Cups

Upright: Compassion, emotional maturity, intuition, empathy, sensitivity and understanding.

Reversed: Emotional overwhelm, insecurity, poor boundaries, dependence or difficulty trusting your intuition.

The Queen of Cups represents emotional depth combined with compassion and self-awareness. She understands feelings without allowing them to completely control her decisions.

Queen of Cups in a Reading

The Queen of Cups can represent a deeply empathetic person or an approach to a situation that requires patience, understanding and emotional awareness.

She may also suggest that your intuition is worth paying attention to. However, emotional sensitivity works best when combined with healthy boundaries and an awareness of your own needs.

Queen of Cups as Advice

Give yourself permission to acknowledge your emotions without judging them. Listen carefully to your intuition, while also checking whether your interpretation is supported by what you actually know.

Compassion does not require you to ignore your own boundaries. You can care deeply about someone while still protecting your own emotional wellbeing.

Questions for Reflection

  • What are my emotions telling me right now?
  • Am I listening to my intuition without letting fear control my interpretation?
  • Where do I need stronger emotional boundaries?
  • How can I show compassion while still looking after myself?

Reflection: The Queen of Cups reminds you that emotional awareness can be a source of strength. Listen to your feelings and intuition while maintaining the boundaries that help you remain grounded.

K

King of Cups

Upright: Emotional balance, maturity, compassion, calm leadership, wisdom and self-control.

Reversed: Emotional repression, instability, manipulation, moodiness or difficulty managing strong feelings.

The King of Cups represents emotional maturity and the ability to experience strong feelings while responding with patience and awareness.

King of Cups in a Reading

The King of Cups can represent someone who is emotionally intelligent, calm and supportive. He may also describe the approach needed in a situation where strong emotions are involved.

Unlike emotional suppression, emotional maturity means being able to recognise and understand feelings without allowing them to dictate every action.

King of Cups as Advice

Give yourself enough space to understand what you feel before reacting. Strong emotions do not have to disappear before you can respond thoughtfully.

If someone around you is struggling emotionally, offer support without taking responsibility for feelings that belong to them.

Questions for Reflection

  • How can I respond rather than react to my emotions?
  • Where could greater emotional balance help me?
  • Am I expressing my feelings honestly and constructively?
  • How can I support others without losing my own emotional boundaries?

Reflection: The King of Cups reminds you that emotional strength does not mean feeling less. It means understanding your emotions well enough to respond with compassion, patience and self-control.
