Build your own Tarot routine

  1. Draw one card in the morning.
  2. Write your first impression before checking a guide.
  3. Use one focused question when something needs deeper reflection.
  4. Record what actually happened instead of forcing the card to fit.
  5. Review your notes weekly and notice repeated themes.

Keep decisions grounded: Tarot can provide a reflective framework, but important health, legal, financial and relationship decisions should also use relevant real-world information and qualified advice.
