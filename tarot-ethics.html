Tarot Ethics and Responsible Readings

Responsible Tarot practice respects privacy, consent and personal choice.

1

Get Consent

If you read for another person, make sure they want a reading and understand what you are offering.

2

Respect Privacy

A card reading is not a reliable way to establish another person's private thoughts, messages or intentions.

3

Avoid Fear-Based Claims

Explain uncertainty and avoid presenting symbolic material as established fact or evidence that something terrible will happen.

4

Repeated Readings

Asking the same question repeatedly because you dislike the first result can create confusion rather than clarity.

5

Health Questions

Tarot should not diagnose illness, choose treatment or replace qualified healthcare professionals.

6

Legal and Financial Questions

Cards cannot establish legal outcomes or guarantee investments. Use documents, evidence and qualified advice.

7

Vulnerable People

Extra care is appropriate when someone is young, distressed or dependent on the reader.

8

Keep Responsibility With the Person

Offer possibilities and questions rather than presenting a card as an unavoidable instruction.

9

The Principle Here

This site treats Tarot as a tool for reflection and learning. Cards can prompt questions but do not replace evidence or personal responsibility.

☾

Keep Tarot Grounded

Tarot readings on this website are for reflection and entertainment. They are not guaranteed predictions and should not replace qualified medical, legal, financial, mental health or other specialist advice.
