Choosing your first tarot deck

If you are new to tarot, start with artwork you can read comfortably and a guidebook that explains the cards clearly. A traditional Rider–Waite–Smith style deck can make it easier to follow many beginner lessons because its imagery is widely discussed, but it is not the only valid choice. Look at sample card images, check the deck language and size, and read the retailer's delivery and returns information before buying.
