Tarot de Marseille · Swords

Page of Swords

Valet de Épées · Épées

Page of Swords combines a learner, messenger or early expression with thought, conflict and discernment. In Marseille readings, its court role, Swords suit, figure’s posture and attributes all contribute to the interpretation.

Tarot de Marseille

French title: Valet de Épées

Keywords: a learner, messenger or early expression, thought, conflict and discernment, clear thinking, decisions and boundaries

Browse the Marseille deck

Upright Meaning

In the Marseille tradition, this card combines a learner, messenger or early expression with thought, conflict and discernment. Curiosity, study and a new message are expressed through clear thinking, decisions and boundaries. Consider how this pattern is developing in the practical circumstances of your question. In a practical reading, consider this question: What is being learned, and how can curiosity stay open but grounded? Test the answer against the court figure’s posture, attributes and the circumstances of the question.

Reversed Meaning

The reversed card can suggest immaturity or information not yet understood alongside confusion, tension or poorly directed judgement. Consider whether the card's central pattern is blocked, excessive, delayed or being experienced inwardly.

What This Card Brings Into Focus

Read this as a Marseille Swords court card: a learner, messenger or early expression is the starting point. What is being learned, and how can curiosity stay open but grounded? Study the figure’s posture and attributes before deciding how the card applies.

Love & Relationships

Page of Swords brings a learner, messenger or early expression into the relationship question. Consider communication, assumptions, boundaries and the willingness to address difficult truths without turning disagreement into a contest. The card cannot establish another person's private thoughts; use it to reflect on observable behaviour and honest communication.

Career & Work

Page of Swords asks how a learner, messenger or early expression affects practical decisions. For work and study, examine decisions, communication, planning and the quality of information available.

Personal Growth

For personal reflection, Page of Swords asks you to examine a learner, messenger or early expression through thought, conflict and discernment. What is being learned, and how can curiosity stay open but grounded?

Symbolism & Visual Study

Study the Marseille court figure closely. Notice posture, gaze, clothing, objects, direction and how the figure occupies the card. Consider how these details express a learner, messenger or early expression, thought, conflict and discernment, clear thinking, decisions and boundaries without assuming the figure represents a specific person.

Reading the Marseille Tradition

The Tarot de Marseille is a family of historical patterns, not one single immutable deck. This guide uses a Marseille-pattern 78-card image set and modern reflective interpretations. Numbered suit cards are read through pip arrangement, number and suit symbolism; they do not use the same illustrated scenes as Rider–Waite–Smith.
