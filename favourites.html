Favourite Cards

Save cards from readings or the card library, then add a private note about why a card keeps calling your attention.

Why Keep a List of Favourite Tarot Cards?

Saving a card can be useful when you are studying recurring themes, building familiarity with the deck or noticing which images consistently attract your attention. A favourite is not a prediction or a judgement about a card; it is simply a private study marker stored in your browser.

Turn favourites into study prompts

For each saved card, consider writing why it stood out. Was it the imagery, the traditional meaning, a reading where it appeared, or a question it raised? Revisit the notes later and compare how your interpretation changes in different contexts.

Your notes stay on this device

This feature uses browser storage rather than a server-side account. That means your saved cards are convenient for personal use but can be lost if the browser's site data is cleared. Do not store sensitive information that you would not want retained in the browser.
