Upright Meaning

In the Marseille tradition, this card combines outward authority and established mastery with material resources, work and practical life. Reversed Meaning

The reversed card can suggest rigidity or misuse of authority alongside worry about resources or misplaced priorities. Consider whether the card's central pattern is blocked, excessive, delayed or being experienced inwardly.

Love & Relationships

King of Pentacles brings outward authority and established mastery into the relationship question. Look at reliability, shared responsibilities, practical support and whether promises are matched by consistent effort. The card cannot establish another person's private thoughts; use it to reflect on observable behaviour and honest communication.

Career & Work

King of Pentacles asks how outward authority and established mastery affects practical decisions. For work and money, examine resources, skill, effort and long-term results. Check costs, commitments and evidence before acting.

Reading the Marseille Tradition

The Tarot de Marseille is a family of historical patterns, not one single immutable deck. This guide uses a Marseille-pattern 78-card image set and modern reflective interpretations. Numbered suit cards are read through pip arrangement, number and suit symbolism; they do not use the same illustrated scenes as Rider–Waite–Smith.
