Tarot Numerology

Numbers provide a useful study pattern for the Minor Arcana without replacing the card image, suit or spread position.

1

Aces — Potential

An opening, seed, invitation or raw potential.

2

Twos — Relationship and Choice

Comparison, balance, partnership or a choice between directions.

3

Threes — Growth and Expression

Development, collaboration and expression as a possibility takes shape.

4

Fours — Structure and Stability

Boundaries, consolidation and stability, which can also become stagnation.

5

Fives — Disruption and Challenge

Tension, disruption or a change to an established pattern.

6

Sixes — Adjustment and Movement

Recovery, exchange, harmony or movement after difficulty.

7

Sevens — Assessment

Evaluation, patience, strategy and a closer look at what is happening.

8

Eights — Development and Direction

Movement, skill, discipline and the consequences of sustained effort.

9

Nines — Culmination

Maturity, independence, pressure or a process approaching completion.

10

Tens — Completion and Transition

A cycle reaching its fullest expression and preparing to change.

11

Number + Suit

Five of Cups and Five of Wands share a number but explore different subjects. Always read the number with the suit and context.

12

Repeated Numbers

Several cards sharing a number can create a theme. Notice it first, then check whether it genuinely fits the question.
