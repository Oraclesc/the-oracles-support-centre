Career Tarot Guide

Career readings can organise thoughts about work and choices, but should sit alongside practical research and evidence.

1

Start With the Real Situation

Write down the opportunity and the facts you know: deadlines, responsibilities, finances, location and qualifications.

2

Questions That Lead to Action

What should I understand? What preparation would help? What strength can I use? What factor am I overlooking? What should I investigate before changing jobs?

3

Four-Card Career Spread

Card 1: current position. Card 2: challenge. Card 3: opportunity worth investigating. Card 4: practical focus for the next step.

4

Separate Symbolism From Evidence

A card about change does not prove you should resign and a card about success does not guarantee a job offer. Research the real situation.

5

After the Reading

Write one thing the cards helped you notice and one fact you still need to investigate.

Keep Tarot Grounded
