Select a card to begin.

How to use this comparison: The Major Arcana share recognisable archetypes, but names, numbering and emphasis can differ. In particular, Marseille commonly places Justice at VIII and Strength (La Force) at XI, while Rider–Waite–Smith swaps those positions. The numbered Marseille suit cards are usually pip designs rather than illustrated scenes, so their meanings are approached through number, suit and pattern—not by assuming they tell the same visual story as Rider–Waite–Smith.
