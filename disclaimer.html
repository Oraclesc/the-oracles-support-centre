Tarot Disclaimer

Last updated: November 2026

The Oracle's Support Centre provides tarot readings, card meanings and reflective exercises for entertainment, education and personal reflection. Tarot is an interpretive practice. The content on this website is not a statement of fact about your life or anyone else's, and it should not be treated as a guaranteed prediction.

Not professional advice

Nothing on this website is medical, mental-health, legal, financial, employment or other professional advice. Tarot readings cannot diagnose illness, assess legal rights, determine whether an investment is suitable, or replace guidance from a qualified professional. For decisions with significant consequences, seek advice from an appropriately qualified person who understands your circumstances.

No guarantees or certainty

Card meanings are symbolic interpretations, not evidence that a particular event will happen. A reading cannot confirm what another person thinks, feels, intends or has done. It cannot guarantee relationship outcomes, employment decisions, financial results or future events. Different readers and tarot traditions may interpret the same card differently.

Personal choice and responsibility

Use the material as a prompt to consider questions, identify assumptions and explore possible perspectives. You are responsible for your own decisions and actions. Do not use a reading as the sole basis for a major decision, and do not let repeated readings replace direct communication, practical research or appropriate support.

Wellbeing and urgent situations

If you are worried about your physical or mental health, speak with a qualified healthcare professional. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than relying on a tarot reading or this website.

Images, history and interpretations

Our guides discuss common interpretations and visual details from named tarot traditions. Symbolic meanings can vary between decks, historical sources and individual readers. Where a page discusses a particular image or tradition, use that context rather than assuming every deck follows the same system.

Entertainment and personal reflection

By using the readings and tools, you acknowledge that they are reflective resources rather than professional services or guaranteed answers. You remain free to accept, question or disregard an interpretation.
