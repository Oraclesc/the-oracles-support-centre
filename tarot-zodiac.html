Tarot & Zodiac

Astrological correspondences are part of several Tarot traditions. They are useful as a study framework, but different schools assign some cards differently.

🌙 Moon & Tarot practice

Instead of treating lunar phases as guarantees, use them as a recurring journaling structure:

  1. New Moon: What intention deserves attention?
  2. Waxing: What am I developing?
  3. Full Moon: What has become clearer?
  4. Waning: What can I release, simplify or stop feeding?

Pair each phase with one card and compare your notes later. The value is in the review, not in assuming the phase causes a particular outcome.
