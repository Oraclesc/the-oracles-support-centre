Tarot de Marseille

French title: Cavalier de Deniers

Keywords: movement, pursuit or an active force, material resources, work and practical life, resources, tangible results and security

Browse the Marseille deck

Upright Meaning

In the Marseille tradition, this card combines movement, pursuit or an active force with material resources, work and practical life. Taking action and carrying an idea forward is expressed through resources, tangible results and security. Consider how this pattern is developing in the practical circumstances of your question.

Reversed Meaning

The reversed card can suggest haste, inconsistency or a stalled pursuit alongside worry about resources or misplaced priorities. Consider whether the card's central pattern is blocked, excessive, delayed or being experienced inwardly.

What This Card Brings Into Focus

The Marseille court uses four ranks: Valet, Cavalier, Reine and Roi. The number and suit work together; surrounding cards and spread position help refine the reading.

Love & Relationships

In relationships, look at reliability, shared responsibilities, practical support and whether promises are matched by consistent effort.

Career & Work

For work and money, this suit is closely connected with practical resources, skill, effort and long-term results. Check costs, commitments and evidence before acting.

Personal Growth

For personal reflection, consider how the card's number and suit combine. Notice the stage of development represented by the number and the area of life described by the suit.

Symbolism & Visual Study

The Marseille court uses four ranks: Valet, Cavalier, Reine and Roi. Count the suit emblems and observe their arrangement, symmetry, decoration and any central flowers or leaves. The pattern itself can contribute to interpretation.

Practical Advice

Begin with the card's number or archetype, then add the suit or visual detail. Relate that combination to the question and spread position. Consider one alternative reading and test both against real circumstances. A card is a reflective prompt, not proof or a guaranteed forecast.

Reading the Marseille Tradition

The Tarot de Marseille is a family of historical patterns, not one single immutable deck. This guide uses a Marseille-pattern 78-card image set and modern reflective interpretations. Numbered suit cards are read through pip arrangement, number and suit symbolism; they do not use the same illustrated scenes as Rider–Waite–Smith.
