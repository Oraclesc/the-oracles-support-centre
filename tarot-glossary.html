Tarot Glossary

Tarot has its own vocabulary. These definitions explain common terms in plain language.

1

Arcana

The two main groups of the Tarot deck: Major Arcana and Minor Arcana.

2

Major Arcana

The 22 numbered cards from The Fool through The World, often used for broad themes and transitions.

3

Minor Arcana

The 56 cards divided into Cups, Pentacles, Swords and Wands, often exploring everyday experiences.

4

Suit

One of the four Minor Arcana groups, each with recurring themes and imagery.

5

Court Card

Page, Knight, Queen and King cards. They may represent people, roles, qualities or approaches.

6

Querent

The person asking a question or receiving a reading.

7

Spread

A layout in which each card has a defined position or role.

8

Reversal

A card appearing upside down. Readers differ on whether reversals have distinct meanings.

9

Clarifier

An optional extra card used to explore another card or question in more detail.

10

Significator

A card deliberately chosen to represent a person, situation or theme.

11

Intuition

A reader's immediate impression or association with a card.

12

Symbolism

The images, objects, colours, numbers and settings that communicate themes.

13

Card Combination

The way two or more cards influence one another through position, contrast and shared themes.

14

Rider–Waite–Smith

A highly influential early-20th-century Tarot deck created by A. E. Waite and illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith.

15

Numerology

The use of number patterns as an additional interpretive layer.

16

Reflection Question

A question designed to encourage examination rather than demand a fixed prediction.

17

Tarot Journal

A record of questions, cards, observations and later reflections.
