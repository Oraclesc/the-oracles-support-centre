Major Arcana · Balance

Temperance

The Temperance brings attention to balance, moderation and combining different needs. Its place in the Major Arcana makes it useful for reflecting on larger patterns, transitions and choices.

Major ArcanaBalance

Keywords: moderation, patience, harmony

Upright Meaning

Combine patience with steady action. Balance and cooperation will produce better results than extremes.

Reversed Meaning

Something is out of balance. Slow down and adjust before the situation becomes harder to manage.

Love & Relationships

In relationships, Temperance can invite reflection on balance, moderation and combining different needs. Look at the behaviour behind the feeling: what is being chosen, avoided, shared or changed?

Career & Work

For work and study, consider where balance, moderation and combining different needs is showing up in your responsibilities. The card can help you name the approach you are taking before deciding whether it still serves the goal.

Personal Growth

For personal growth, use Temperance as a question rather than a verdict. Where are you being asked to practise balance, moderation and combining different needs, and what would a small real-world example look like today?

Symbolism & Intuition

The artwork of Temperance rewards slow observation. Start with the strongest visual element and ask what it suggests about balance, moderation and combining different needs. Compare your first impression with the traditional meaning rather than replacing one with the other.

Reflection Questions

  • Where is balance, moderation and combining different needs showing up in my life right now?
  • What does the Temperance make me notice that I was overlooking?
  • What practical choice would let me explore this card without treating it as a fixed prediction?

Practical advice: Let the card suggest a perspective, then test that perspective against your actual circumstances and values.
