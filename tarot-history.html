A Brief History of Tarot

Understanding Tarot's history helps separate documented origins from later traditions and popular myths.

1

Tarot Began as a Card Game

The earliest known Tarot-like decks appeared in northern Italy during the 15th century as luxury playing-card packs. They were not originally created as a fortune-telling system.

2

The Structure Developed Over Time

The familiar 78-card structure combines four suits with a special group now called the Major Arcana. Early decks varied in imagery, names and composition.

3

Divination Came Later

Using Tarot for divination became documented centuries after the cards appeared. Later occult writers developed theories connecting Tarot with ancient wisdom and other systems.

4

Rider–Waite–Smith

The Rider–Waite–Smith deck was first published in 1909. Arthur Edward Waite provided the concepts and Pamela Colman Smith created the illustrations.

5

Modern Tarot

Today Tarot is used as a card game, artistic tradition, symbolic system, spiritual practice and reflective tool. Readers do not all agree on meanings.

6

History vs Tradition

Claims that Tarot originated in ancient Egypt are popular in some traditions but are not established by evidence about the cards' origins.

7

Why History Helps

Historical context shows that Tarot meanings developed over time. You can learn traditional meanings while developing a thoughtful personal interpretation.
