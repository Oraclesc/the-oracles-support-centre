Tarot de Marseille

French title: Le Jugement

Keywords: awakening, response, renewal

Browse the Marseille deck

Upright Meaning

Le Jugement depicts a call to awaken, answer and renew. It can suggest recognition, a message or a moment when a decision can no longer be postponed.

Reversed Meaning

A call may be ignored through fear or answered without reflection. Listen carefully, review the past and decide what response is responsible now.

What This Card Brings Into Focus

As a Marseille trump, Le Jugement is best approached through its own image, number 20, and place in the sequence. Its key tension is awakening, response, renewal.

Love & Relationships

Judgement brings awakening into the relationship question. Consider the choices, values and patterns that shape the connection. The card cannot establish another person's private thoughts; use it to reflect on observable behaviour and honest communication.

Career & Work

Judgement asks how awakening affects practical decisions. For work, study or major projects, consider direction, responsibility and the wider stage of development.

Personal Growth

For personal reflection, consider how awakening challenges your current approach. What value, habit or choice is being brought into view?

Symbolism & Visual Study

Study the actual Marseille image of Le Jugement before importing meanings from a modern illustrated deck. Notice the figures, posture, objects, colours and direction. In particular, test how the image expresses awakening, response, renewal.

Reading the Marseille Tradition

The Tarot de Marseille is a family of historical patterns, not one single immutable deck. This guide uses a Marseille-pattern 78-card image set and modern reflective interpretations. Numbered suit cards are read through pip arrangement, number and suit symbolism; they do not use the same illustrated scenes as Rider–Waite–Smith.
