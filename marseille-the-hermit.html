Tarot de Marseille · Major Arcana

The Hermit

L’Hermite · Major Arcana

The Hermit is Marseille trump 9. Its image and place in the sequence are read through prudence, experience, reflection.

Tarot de Marseille

French title: L’Hermite

Keywords: prudence, experience, reflection

Browse the Marseille deck

Upright Meaning

L’Hermite carries a lantern of experience. He suggests patient inquiry, discretion and wisdom gained by taking time.

Reversed Meaning

Withdrawal can become stagnation, or caution can prevent a necessary step. Decide what you are waiting to understand.

What This Card Brings Into Focus

As a Marseille trump, L’Hermite is best approached through its own image, number 9, and place in the sequence. Its key tension is prudence, experience, reflection.

Love & Relationships

The Hermit brings prudence into the relationship question. Consider the choices, values and patterns that shape the connection. The card cannot establish another person's private thoughts; use it to reflect on observable behaviour and honest communication.

Career & Work

The Hermit asks how prudence affects practical decisions. For work, study or major projects, consider direction, responsibility and the wider stage of development.

Personal Growth

For personal reflection, consider how prudence challenges your current approach. What value, habit or choice is being brought into view?

Symbolism & Visual Study

Study the actual Marseille image of L’Hermite before importing meanings from a modern illustrated deck. Notice the figures, posture, objects, colours and direction. In particular, test how the image expresses prudence, experience, reflection.

Worked example: reading The Hermit in context

Practice question: “What larger choice, lesson or turning point does The Hermit bring into view?” This fictional prompt is deliberately open-ended.

Step 1 — Start with the Marseille meaning: L’Hermite carries a lantern of experience. He suggests patient inquiry, discretion and wisdom gained by taking time.

Step 2 — Notice the system: This is a Major Arcana card, so begin with its archetypal image, number and traditional role in the sequence. The keywords prudence, experience, reflection are prompts, not a checklist.

Step 3 — Test another angle: Withdrawal can become stagnation, or caution can prevent a necessary step. Decide what you are waiting to understand. A reversal is one possible reading method, not a universal Marseille rule; some readers use upright cards only.

Step 4 — Connect it to life: Begin with the Marseille image of L’Hermite and its themes of prudence, experience, reflection. Relate its place in the sequence to the question, then consider what evidence would support or challenge your interpretation. L’Hermite carries a lantern of experience. Step 5 — Write a grounded response: “The theme I want to examine is ______. One practical thing I can observe or do is ______.” Keep the action within your control.

Reading the Marseille Tradition

The Tarot de Marseille is a family of historical patterns, not one single immutable deck. This guide uses a Marseille-pattern 78-card image set and modern reflective interpretations. Numbered suit cards are read through pip arrangement, number and suit symbolism; they do not use the same illustrated scenes as Rider–Waite–Smith.
